# Synthetic Formatting Guide

This file is a privacy-safe sample. It is not copied from any real university, journal, or publisher template.

## Page Setup

- Paper size: A4.
- Margins: top 2.54 cm, bottom 2.54 cm, left 3.17 cm, right 2.54 cm.
- Header: use "本科毕业论文" in Songti, 9 pt.
- Footer: centered page number in Songti, 9 pt.

## Body Text

- Chinese body text: Songti, 12 pt, 1.25 line spacing.
- English body text: Times New Roman, 12 pt, 1.25 line spacing.
- First-line indent: 2 Chinese characters.
- Alignment: justified.

## Sections

- Chinese abstract is required.
- Chinese keywords are required.
- English abstract is required.
- English keywords are required.
- Table of contents is required.

## Headings

- Level 1 heading: Heiti, 16 pt, bold, centered.
- Level 2 heading: Heiti, 14 pt, bold, left aligned.
- Level 3 heading: Heiti, 12 pt, bold, left aligned.

## Captions

- Figure caption: Songti, 10.5 pt, centered.
- Table caption: Songti, 10.5 pt, centered.

## Length

- Main text should be at least 10,000 non-space characters.
